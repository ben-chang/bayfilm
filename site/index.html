About BayFilm

Thanks for using BayFilm! This website gathers showtimes from the San Francisco Bay Area's independent movie theaters into one guide. Listings are indexed nightly from each theater's calendar and linked to their box offices. Be sure to check out events from SFFilm and SFMoMA as well! Thanks for using and hope you enjoy!
