What's playing at San Francisco Bay Area independent movie theaters

BayFilm gathers showtimes from the San Francisco Bay Area's independent movie theaters into one guide. Listings are indexed nightly from each theater's calendar and linked to their box offices. Be sure to check out events from SFFilm and SFMoMA as well! See also Spiral HWY, an excellent film aggregation site. Thanks for using and hope you enjoy!

A few theaters are hard to index, so their listings aren't included here. Please check their websites directly: The New Parkway (Oakland) · Rialto Cinemas Elmwood (Berkeley) · Rialto Cinemas Cerrito (El Cerrito)

Some theaters, including the Stanford Theatre and BAMPFA, block automated indexing, so their listings refresh less often than the nightly update. When a theater's listings are more than a day old, they're marked with a "listings from" badge, so double-check those with the box office.
